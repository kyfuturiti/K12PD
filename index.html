About Futuriti

Built by Kentucky. Built for Kentucky.

Futuriti is a free college and career planning platform built by the Kentucky Council on Postsecondary Education (CPE). Everything in it is powered by real Kentucky data — not national averages. Students can explore 700+ careers, discover in-demand jobs in their region, find pathways at Kentucky institutions, and get matched to scholarships and careers based on their strengths. And it's 100% free for students, families, and educators across the Commonwealth.
